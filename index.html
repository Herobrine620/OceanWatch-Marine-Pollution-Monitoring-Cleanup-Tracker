04 OCEAN Marine ecosystem

Ocean pollution is a system problem involving production, consumption, waste management, runoff, rivers, industry, fishing and human behavior.

The project separates pollution pressure from measured ocean leakage . There is no single scientifically defensible annual percentage series for “total ocean pollution” from 2000–2026.

04 / 2000—2026

A timeline of
Changes Ocean Pollution.

2000–05

Plastic pollution rises

Plastic waste begins accumulating along coasts, rivers and marine environments.

2005–10

Coastal hotspots emerge

More polluted locations are recorded, making recurring waste zones visible.

2010–15

Microplastic concern grows

Research and monitoring reveal increasing levels of tiny plastic particles in water.

2015–20

Cleanup efforts expand

Beach cleanups, citizen reporting and community monitoring become more common.

2020–25

Pollution monitoring improves

Satellite imagery, mapping and digital reporting make polluted areas easier to track.

2026

Data-driven cleanup

Photos, locations and waste measurements connect pollution evidence directly to action.

Ocean pollution includes plastics, sewage, nutrients, oil, chemicals, fishing gear and other pollutants measured differently.
